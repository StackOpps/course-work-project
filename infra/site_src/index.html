Our promise

Why CraftHaven

Handmade, always

No mass production. Every listing is made to order or in small, numbered batches.

Independent makers

Makers keep the majority of every sale — CraftHaven takes a small, transparent fee.

Fair, transparent pricing

You see what the maker earns. No hidden markups between the workshop and your door.

About us

Built for makers who got tired of marketplaces built for everyone else

CraftHaven started among three friends who make and sell their own work — pottery, woodwork and textiles. We were frustrated by fees that ate into every sale and storefronts that made handmade goods look identical to anything else online.

So we're building something smaller and more deliberate: a storefront that gives independent makers room to tell the story behind what they make, and gives buyers confidence that a real person made it.
